# MMC

## Installation Security Alert

1. Trigger the warning.
    Double-click the .pkg file to attempt opening it, then click Done or Cancel
    when the security alert appears.
    
2. Open Privacy & Security.
    Click the Apple menu () in the top-left corner, choose System Settings (or
    System Preferences on older macOS versions), and select Privacy & Security.

3. Click Open Anyway.
    Scroll down to the Security section where your blocked .pkg file is listed,
    then click Open Anyway next to it.

4. Confirm and install.
    Click Open (or Open Anyway) on the confirmation pop-up, enter your Mac's
    administrator password or use Touch ID, and the installer will launch.
